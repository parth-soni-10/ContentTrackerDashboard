# Composition Brief — 📺 Content Tracker

## Story arc
A watchlist usually lives as a messy list nobody can make sense of. This dashboard reads the watchlist live from a Google Sheet and turns it into charts — what's watched, on which platform, which genre, year by year.

## Visual language
- **Palette:** forest greens `#2d6a4f` / `#74c69d` on cream `#f4f8f5`, with a deep ink scene for the dashboard
- **Accent:** gold `#c97d3a` for highlights
- **Type:** Inter (site font), heavy weights for display, tabular numerals
- **Motifs:** platform color dots (Netflix red, Prime blue, Disney+ blue), watchlist KPIs, genre bars

## Scenes (30s, soft crossfades between scenes)

1. **Hook (0–7s)** — cream. Eyebrow "📺 CONTENT TRACKER"; giant "WATCHLIST," + green "tracked." + platform dots (Netflix, Prime, Disney+).
2. **Proof (6.2–13.8s)** — cream. "My watchlist, turned into pictures and numbers. Live from a Google Sheet." Pills: PLATFORMS · GENRES · YEARS.
3. **The Work (13–23s)** — ink. Dashboard card: "Content Tracker · Live · Sheets" with KPI rows (342 Titles, 1,640 Hours, 205 Shows) and platform bars (Netflix, Prime, Disney+).
4. **Philosophy (22.2–27s)** — cream. "If I log it, it shows up. No manual updates."
5. **Outro (26.2–30s)** — ink. "CONTENT TRACKER" + "Your watchlist, quantified."
