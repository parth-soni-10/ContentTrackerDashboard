Content TrackerPersonal media watchlist & analytics dashboard

Content Tracker is a personal media watchlist and analytics dashboard. Every film and series is logged once — with its genre, streaming platform, episode count, screentime and watch date — and the dashboard turns that history into the numbers behind the viewing.

Every film and series I watch, measured

The watchlist holds roughly 380 entries, one per film or per season of television. Nothing is estimated: an entry exists because it was watched, and its screentime is the minutes spent on it. From those rows the site answers the questions a list cannot.

What each entry records

A title, its season, whether it is a film or a series, its genre, the platform it was watched on, the episode count, the screentime in minutes and the watch date. Future-dated rows are planned watches; they appear in the Data page and the calendar but are kept out of the “recently watched” figures.

Questions the dashboard answers

  • How many titles and hours were watched this year, and on which platforms?
  • Which genres dominate the watchlist, and which months were the heaviest?
  • How does this year compare with the same months of the previous year?
  • Which films and episodes are releasing on the streaming services the watchlist follows?
  • Which unwatched title should come next, picked at random from the list?

The pages

Current Year and All Time carry the charts; Data is the searchable, sortable table with CSV export; Timeline is the history month by month; Calendar is the release calendar; Random Pick chooses from the watchlist; Submit is the suggestion box. The navigation above is the same list, and any page can be linked directly by its hash.

Reading this site as an agent

Every page has a Markdown representation. Send Accept: text/markdown to this URL and the response is Markdown with Content-Type: text/markdown and Vary: Accept; the static copies are at /index.md, /about.md, /contact.md and /privacy.md. llms.txt explains when this site is worth using and how to read the public watchlist JSON; the sitemap lists every page; and the JSON-LD in this page's head describes the site, its author and the application.

About, contact and privacy

Content Tracker is built and maintained by Parth Shirish Soni, a data analyst in Dublin. The contact page explains how to report a correction or an accessibility problem, and the privacy page lists exactly what the site stores — including the local storage keys it uses and the third parties it fetches media metadata from.
