Contact Content Tracker

Corrections, accessibility reports, broken links and questions about the data

Email is the way to reach me

Content Tracker is a personal project maintained by Parth Shirish Soni in Dublin, Ireland. There is no support desk and no ticket system - email is read by the person who builds and maintains the site.

Email: soni.soni.parth@gmail.com
Code and issues: github.com/parth-soni-10/ContentTrackerDashboard
Other projects: parth-portfolio-wb.pages.dev

What to include

A specific report gets a specific answer. These are the details that make one possible:

  • A data correction: the page URL, the title and season, and the field you believe is wrong - genre, platform, episode count, screentime or watch date. A link to a source that shows the correct value helps more than a description.
  • An accessibility problem: the page, the browser or assistive technology you used, and what happened instead of what you expected.
  • A broken link or failed request: the URL and the time you tried it, so the server logs can be matched.
  • A security report: email it rather than opening a public issue, and include the steps to reproduce. Please do not test against the admin panel, and do not write data you do not own.

What I can and cannot help with

Questions about how a figure is calculated, why a title is or is not in the watchlist, where the release calendar gets its data, or how the site is built are all fair game. The code is public, so a question about a particular file can point at the repository.

The site does not sell anything and has no accounts, so there is nothing to sign up for and nothing to cancel. It also cannot add a title to the watchlist on request: suggested titles belong on the Submit page, which stores them separately from the watched list.

Response times and privacy

This is a personal project maintained alongside other work, so expect a reply within a few days rather than within hours. A clear, reproducible report is the fastest route to a useful answer.

Email is received by the site's author and is not added to a mailing list. The privacy page lists what the site itself stores and for how long.
